# TORCH - Brand Lore

## The story

Before a torch was a symbol, it was a tool: you raised it when the path ahead was dark and you needed to keep moving.

TORCH brings that idea to the desktop. A request begins as a spark - something the user intends but has not yet done. TORCH carries that spark through windows, files, apps, and tools, lighting every step until the work returns as a finished result. The path stays visible, so automation never becomes a black box.

TORCH is the light between what you mean and what your computer does.

## What the logo means

- TORCH has no separate mascot or symbol: the name itself is the identity.
- The broad, solid letterforms represent control and reliability.
- The cut terminals and open geometry suggest instructions moving through a system.
- The custom R is the point of transformation, where a familiar wordmark begins to feel active and computational.
- The consistent horizontal rhythm expresses many desktop tools working as one system.

## The visual language

- Warm paper white keeps the product calm and human.
- Near-black represents control, focus, precision, and trust.
- Muted gray gives secondary information a clear place without visual noise.
- Blue is reserved for active work and motion inside the product.

## Canon lines

Primary: **Your desktop. One command. Already moving.**

Brand promise: **Tell TORCH. Consider it done.**

Product principle: **One command. Every step visible.**

Suggested X bio: **Desktop AI that acts. One command in; every step stays visible.**
