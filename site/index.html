What it can do

Find anything

Ask for a file the way you'd describe it to a person. TORCH searches your folders and opens it.

Write and send email

Draft a reply, send a follow-up, check what came in. It shows you the message before it goes.

Use your apps for you

Opens programs and clicks through them the way you would — forms, settings, the bits nobody enjoys.

Speak instead of typing

Press Ctrl+Shift+Space and talk. Your voice is turned into text on your own computer.

Ask before anything risky

Sending, deleting, buying, running commands — TORCH stops and waits for you to say yes.

Undo what it did

Moved the wrong file? One button puts it back.

How it works

01

Say what you want

Type it or say it. "Find the invoice from Tunde and email it to accounts."

02

Watch it work

TORCH breaks the job into steps and shows each one as it goes. A blue border appears whenever it is controlling your screen, so you always know.

03

Approve anything that matters

Before it sends, deletes, or spends, it stops and asks. You can edit what it was about to do, or cancel.
